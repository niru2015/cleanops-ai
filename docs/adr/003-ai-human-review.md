# 003 — AI suggestions with human approval

Status: accepted design baseline, implementation pending. Date: 2026-09-14.

## Decision

Deterministic resolution first; structured AI suggestions; human quality approval and consequential actions.

## Alternatives and rationale

Autonomous approval based on a confidence number cannot establish factual cleanliness or causation.

## Consequences

Supervisor work remains; record overrides and evaluate cost/quality before enabling live models.

## Revisit when

A separately approved, evaluated low-impact automation policy.
