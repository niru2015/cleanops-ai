## 10-minute presenter route (read only)

1. **Sign in (1 minute).** Open <https://cleanops-ai.vercel.app/login>. Choose **Darrel — Director · finance showcase** from the Finance showcase group, enter the protected scenario password, and sign in. Show the “Prototype • synthetic data” banner. The older Director account belongs to a different organization and cannot see this scenario.
2. **Start with a complete month (2 minutes).** Open `/finance?month=2026-08&siteId=a04f9260-4daf-5f07-aa75-cfcca042e764`. Grand Villa shows CAD 1,751.97 recognized revenue, CAD 81.00 direct labour and CAD 1,670.97 direct contribution, with a complete import and current closed period. Explain that expected contract revenue and recognized accounting revenue are separate, and direct contribution excludes overhead, depreciation and tax.
3. **Compare a different cost mix (1 minute).** In the finance overview, select River Rock for August. It shows CAD 950.00 recognized revenue, CAD 73.65 supplies, CAD 93.93 repairs and CAD 782.42 direct contribution. Both sites' figures come from accepted source rows. Selecting **All assigned casinos** displays four cards, but its combined total is **N/A** because Parq and Scenario Site 4 have no accepted August import. Do not present a combined margin from that control.
4. **Trace contract obligations (1 minute).** Open `/finance/contracts`, then `DEMO-FINANCE-SHOWCASE`. The review page shows active version 2, the synthetic amendment PDF, October 1 future effective date, fixed monthly CAD 1,927.17, three weekday positions, a quarterly deep clean and responsibility flags. Say that a Director-approved version creates the operational and expected-revenue rules. The current review page exposes the amendment source; the original version/source is not shown in this view, so do not claim a complete on-screen version-history comparison.
5. **Trace intake and approval (1 minute).** Open `/finance/inbox`. The synthetic WhatsApp candidate preserves its original text and receipt. One duplicate fuel candidate visibly says the exact receipt is already posted and cannot post twice. Open `/finance/expenses` to show the approved August supply and repair receipts and their site allocations. These are replayed synthetic messages, not proof that an existing Tornado WhatsApp group is connected.
6. **Trace labour and project contribution (1 minute).** Open `/finance/time?siteId=a04f9260-4daf-5f07-aa75-cfcca042e764` to point out approved hours and two review exceptions. Open `/finance/rates` only as Director to show effective rates and the older rate interval; posted cost retains its original snapshot. On `/finance/projects`, `HASTINGS-DEEP-CLEAN` has CAD 828.90 accounting-recognized revenue, CAD 159.46 direct cost and CAD 669.44 recognized contribution in the generated manifest. `HASTINGS-REPAIR-PENDING` deliberately leaves recognized contribution pending.
7. **Show the close discipline (1 minute).** Open `/finance/reconciliation`. August is closed and balanced for Grand Villa and River Rock; July is closed but marked stale after a later change; June is open with unmatched operational and accounting amounts. Explain that an accepted CSV is an accounting source and a Director must resolve/close exceptions. Do not describe it as a live Sage connection.
8. **Show the access boundary and invite feedback (2 minutes).** In separate browser sessions, choose **Shayana — Area Manager · Grand Villa**, **Darrel — Area Manager · River Rock**, **Hardeep — Supervisor · finance showcase**, and **Scenario Client Viewer** with the protected scenario password. Each Area Manager sees only the assigned site's August aggregate and no rate detail; Supervisor and Client are denied finance. Ask Tornado to confirm approval owners/thresholds, Sage edition and sample export, source of worker cost rates, receipt/contract retention, and who owns accounting close and the contribution definition.

Avoid creating drafts, approving costs, importing CSVs or resetting the scenario during a customer meeting. Those actions change the shared hosted dataset and need the controlled UAT below.
