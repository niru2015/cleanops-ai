## Design system

- Cool-white canvas; deep navy navigation; restrained cyan/teal accent.
- Humanist system sans serif with compact control typography and strong page hierarchy.
- 8–10px control radii, hairline borders and one subtle drawer shadow.
- Desktop: fixed 254px navigation rail and open workspace.
- Mobile: 68px header, persistent prototype strip and a right-side menu drawer.
- One abstract evidence-document motif; no photos, metrics, data cards or provider branding.
- Motion is limited to the drawer entrance and respects reduced-motion preference.

The concept board includes device/window framing for presentation. Those frames are not
part of the application UI; the implemented shell begins at the navigation and workspace.
